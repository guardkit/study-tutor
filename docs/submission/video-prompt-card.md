# Video Prompt Card — 3 Minutes Max

**Rule: say each bullet, cut to the next. No elaboration. Speed is impact.**

---

## 3. RICH TO CAMERA — Why it's better (15 sec)

- "It doesn't give answers. It teaches."
- "Socratic questioning — it guides her to the answer herself."
- "Watch what happens when she tries to get it to just tell her..."

---

## 4. SCREEN — LilyMay using it + anti-cheat (60 sec)

- **Show LilyMay at the laptop, Open WebUI visible**

- Model responds with Socratic questioning — guiding, not telling
- **The money shot:** She asks "just tell me the answer"
- Model refuses and redirects: scaffolds the thinking instead
- Quick cut to a second subject (History) to show multi-subject

## Production notes
- LilyMay on camera is the emotional hook — prioritise her footage
- The anti-cheat moment is the single most compelling thing for judges
- Reachy is visually memorable — even 10 seconds makes the submission stand out
- Architecture diagrams are credibility, not entertainment — flash them fast
- End on the human impact line, not the tech
