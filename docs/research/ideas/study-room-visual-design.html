02 · the front door

The room is the home screen

Lilymay's layout, mocked with emoji as stand-in sprites (Flux replaces them). HUD top: level + XP, coins, flame — only numbers that grow. Mission pop-up bottom-left. Tapping the desk starts a session. Dark theme isn't a palette swap — it's evening in the room: the sky goes to night, the lamp comes on, the cat sleeps.

03 · the shop

Coins → things → home

Multi-page shop with a weekly featured shelf — rotation copy says “back soon”, never “last chance”. Set trackers open endowed at 1-of-6 (“your pet found the first piece”). Prices in coins only; nothing here ever costs real money.

06 · art direction

Sticker sprites, one shelf of rules

Every generated asset follows the same five rules so a September sprite and an April sprite sit on one shelf. Emoji in these mockups are placeholders at final sprite scale.

Flat sticker, soft edge

Flat 2D shapes, rounded silhouettes, 2px warm-brown outline (#4A3410 at 60%), no gradients except one soft top-light.

Room palette only

Each sprite draws from its room's four material hexes plus indigo #4B5C92 and gold #B98A2E. No colour outside the swatch.

One shadow rule

Single soft ellipse shadow under every grounded object, 12% black, no cast direction — objects must be free to move.

Silhouette first

Every sprite must read at 48px. If it needs detail to be legible, simplify the shape, not the size.

Pets have faces, furniture doesn't

Only living things get eyes. A cheerful lamp is one step from a guilt-tripping lamp — furniture stays furniture.

08 · two rooms, two moods

Detailed watercolour vs. dark academia

Round two, commissioned by the designer: the storybook watercolour set with much more detail (cat, rooster and bookshelf especially), and a second set chasing the ornate boarding-school drama of her reference — carved dark wood, gold filigree, heavy velvet curtains, candlelight. Original artwork aiming for that mood, not a copy of any game. 17 renders, 12 steps, all receipts kept; the armchair, chandelier, coin and more are on disk.
