## What the smoke proves

1. **The anchorless-degradation fix works on the REAL path.** The correct
   Macbeth quotes (raven, dagger) came back as **anchorless primary
   matches WITHOUT a citation — not a strip, not an exception**. Verbatim
   rewritten output for the raven item:

   > Lady Macbeth hears the news and says "The raven himself is hoarse
   > That croaks the fatal entrance of Duncan" — the raven is an omen of
   > death announcing the king's arrival.

   `verifier: primary_matches=1, anchorless_primary_matches=1,
   no_match_strips=0, verifier_exception=False`. Pre-fix, this exact input
   raised `ValueError("…no citation_anchor; corpus loader contract
   broken")` and failed the safety gate open at the coach-handover
   broad-except.

2. **The known fabrication is stripped and counted.** The observed
   2026-04-21 fabrication ("That tend on mortal coats… unmaculate me from
   the deed") scored best windowed similarity **0.515** (fabricated), and
   the verifier stripped the quote marks with the certainty softener:

   > Lady Macbeth calls on the spirits with perhaps That tend on mortal
   > coats… unmaculate me from the deed as she rejects her femininity.

3. **The false-correction scaffold fires where it should.** The
   innocent-flower control (canonical Folger wording) was **stripped by
   the runtime verifier** (edit distance 4: the store's mid-line
   page-number artifact `15` + `under't`) even though the harness metric
   scored it **0.958 = genuinely present** — flagged as
   `no_match_strip` with `corpus_ratio 0.958, chunk 49`. That is the
   "genuine quote lost to a store artifact" class the scaffold exists to
   surface, and a concrete cost of the 2026-05-10 docling ingest that
   Track B1 would repair.

4. **Whole-turn honesty:** retrieval ran for real on every item
   (`retrieval_skipped_reason=None`), no verifier exception anywhere, and
   citation coverage is **0%** — the shipped store's honest 581/581
   anchorless state, measurable now via the additive `anchorless_*`
   counters.
